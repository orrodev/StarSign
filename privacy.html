STARSIGN Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 22, 2026

STARSIGN ("STARSIGN," "we," "us," or "our") operates the STARSIGN application and related services. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how you can manage your information.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use STARSIGN, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Username
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Date of birth
  • Country or region
  • Profile information and photographs
  • Order and transaction information
  • Shipping information
  • Messages and information submitted with autograph requests

2. Celebrity Information

Celebrities applying for a STARSIGN account may be required to provide additional information, including legal name, display or stage name, date of birth, phone number, country, social media information, official website information, identity verification information, and payout information.

3. Identity Verification

STARSIGN may require celebrity applicants to complete identity verification before receiving a verified celebrity account or accepting orders.

Identity verification may be performed by third-party verification and payment providers. Verification may require government-issued identification and other information necessary to confirm identity.

4. Payment Information

Payments and payouts may be processed by third-party payment providers. These providers may collect payment, identity, fraud-prevention, tax, and payout information necessary to provide their services.

STARSIGN does not need to store your complete payment card number when payment processing is handled by a third-party payment provider.

5. How We Use Information

  • Create and manage accounts
  • Process autograph requests and orders
  • Process payments and payouts
  • Verify celebrity identities
  • Facilitate shipping and delivery
  • Provide customer support
  • Prevent fraud and unauthorized activity
  • Maintain and improve STARSIGN
  • Protect the security of the Service
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Enforce our Terms of Service

6. Information Sharing

We may share information with service providers that help us operate STARSIGN, including providers for payment processing, identity verification, hosting, authentication, shipping, customer support, security, and fraud prevention.

We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, and security of STARSIGN, our users, or others.

We do not sell personal information for money.

7. Public Profiles

Information you choose to display on a public STARSIGN profile may be visible to other users. Do not publish information you do not want other users to see.

8. Shipping Information

For physical autograph orders, information necessary to complete shipping may be shared with the appropriate parties involved in fulfilling the order. This may include names, shipping addresses, and tracking information.

9. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, maintain transaction records, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with legal obligations.

10. Account Deletion

You may initiate account deletion through the STARSIGN app. When an account is deleted, we will delete personal information that we are not legally required to retain.

Certain transaction, payment, tax, fraud-prevention, security, or legal records may need to be retained when required by law.

11. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

12. Children's Privacy

STARSIGN is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

13. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain uses of your information.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date above.
