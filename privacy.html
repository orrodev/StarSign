Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This policy describes information handled by the current STARSIGN mobile app and its connected services. It does not describe records held independently by Apple, RevenueCat, Clerk, or Replit.

Information we handle

  • Account and sign-in: Clerk handles account credentials, email verification, and sign-in security. STARSIGN’s API stores your account identifier, role, and any display name submitted to it.
  • Digital requests: STARSIGN stores the selected talent and service, recipient name, request note, order status, and purchase transaction identifiers. Talent receives the request details needed to accept, decline, and fulfill an order.
  • Talent applications: If submitted, stage name, country, category, response estimate, biography, service descriptions, and prices are stored for review. An application is not identity verification or an approved public listing.
  • Device-only information: Favorites and some application details—including legal name, date of birth, contact details, social profile, audience information, and selected photo or video—are stored in local app storage. Selected media files are not uploaded by this release. Other order information may be cached on your device.
  • Safety: Reports can include a reason, details, and a snapshot of related profile or order content. Blocking stores the profile/account identifiers needed to hide that profile and prevent new interactions.
  • Purchases: Apple processes App Store payments. RevenueCat and STARSIGN handle purchase status and product/transaction identifiers. STARSIGN does not receive your payment-card details.

How we use information

We use information to provide account access, process and display digital requests, review talent applications, investigate safety reports, enforce the Terms, and respond to support requests. STARSIGN does not sell personal information or use this release for cross-app advertising tracking. No advertising network or analytics provider is connected in this release.

Service providers and sharing

Clerk provides sign-in; Replit hosts the API and database; Apple and RevenueCat process digital in-app purchases. Information is shared with talent only as needed to handle a request. Physical-item checkout, talent payouts, identity-verification-provider requests, and media uploads are unavailable in this release. Older physical-order or payment references may remain in historical records. Each provider may retain its own records under its policies.

Account deletion and retention

Delete your account in the app from Profile → Delete account. Confirming deletion cancels unfinished requests, removes the Clerk account, and scrubs account-linked personal request or profile content. For an order you placed, recipient and note content are removed. If you owned a talent profile, the profile is anonymized and its services are removed; another collector’s request details may remain with the cancelled order.

If Apple has charged for a cancelled request, deletion does not issue an App Store refund. Request any refund from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com .

We may keep a reduced order/transaction record where needed for legal, tax, accounting, or payment-dispute obligations. Resolved reports submitted by a deleted account are removed. If a report is still under review, its reporter identifier and free-text details are removed while limited evidence may remain for review. For reports about a deleted account, we remove the direct account identifier; report details or evidence may remain where needed to protect users or investigate a safety matter. Apple, RevenueCat, Clerk, and Replit may retain their own records or backups.

Children

STARSIGN is for people aged 13 or older. Do not create an account or use the service if you are under 13.
