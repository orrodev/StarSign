Help & Support

Contact us about account access, digital requests, safety, or privacy.

Contact support

Email orro.dev.team@gmail.com .

Include the email on your STARSIGN account, the order number if relevant, and a short description. Do not email passwords, payment-card numbers, or verification codes.

Digital orders and refunds

A Celebrity Autograph request includes one signed digital portrait. Collectors may request a short personal note from the talent at the same price. The talent-set turnaround estimate appears before purchase. A talent may accept or decline.

Apple handles App Store billing. STARSIGN cannot issue App Store refunds. If a charged request is declined or cancelled, submit a request directly to Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com .

Account deletion

Delete your account inside the app: Profile → Delete account.

Unfinished requests will be cancelled. Deletion does not automatically refund an Apple purchase; use Apple’s refund process for billing requests.

Safety

Use the Report or Block controls on a talent profile, or open Safety Center from your profile.

For an urgent safety concern, email support with the relevant profile or order details.
